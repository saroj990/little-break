AdPause

A proof of concept for Amazon Prime Video and Hotstar (including JioHotstar). It watches the player for an advertisement, covers the picture with a short information card, and mutes ad audio until the ad ends.

It does not skip or speed up advertisements, and it does not touch DRM or network requests.

On Prime Video, detection uses visible player UI from the ATV Web Player SDK, including atvwebplayersdk-ad-timer. On Hotstar, it uses the separate ad video in ad-video-container. If those signals are absent, the overlay stays hidden.

Use “Simulate ad” in the toolbar popup to preview the overlay without a live ad. That path is labeled as a simulation.
